# Enfield Royal Clinic Dubai – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Enfield Royal Clinic L.L.C. is a Dubai-based healthcare and medical aesthetics provider established in 2007. The clinic offers a wide range of surgical and non-surgical treatments, including plastic surgery, dermatology, cosmetic dentistry, hair restoration, wellness and multidisciplinary medical services.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting the clinic's extensive services in a more organised way, strengthening its specialist-led positioning and improving the journey from treatment discovery to consultation enquiries.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Simplified Treatment Navigation

- Organises treatments into six clear categories: Plastic Surgery, Aesthetic Treatments, Dermatology, Hair Restoration, Dental Care and Wellness.
- Reduces the complexity of the extensive service navigation.
- Makes it easier for visitors to identify relevant treatments and explore available services.

**Issue addressed:** The original website presented a very broad treatment menu that could overwhelm visitors and make service discovery difficult.

### 2. Stronger Homepage Positioning and First Impression

- Introduces a clearer headline communicating the clinic's medical and aesthetic expertise.
- Strengthens the presentation of the clinic's specialist-led services.
- Gives greater prominence to consultation and appointment calls to action.

**Issue addressed:** The original homepage did not communicate the clinic's complete specialist offering and value proposition clearly enough to guide prospective patients.

### 3. Improved Credibility and Trust Presentation

- Removes incomplete or unreliable statistic counters such as "0k+".
- Prioritises verified clinic information and medical specialist credentials.
- Uses authentic testimonials, verified reviews or confirmed experience figures only where supporting information is available.

**Issue addressed:** Incomplete homepage statistics weakened the website's professional appearance and could make visitors question the accuracy of its claims.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Creates a more professional medical and aesthetic appearance aligned with the clinic's specialist positioning.
- **Layout and Hierarchy:** Establishes a clearer homepage introduction, organised treatment categories and more visible consultation actions.
- **User Experience:** Reduces navigation complexity and makes important services easier to explore.
- **Content Consistency:** Improves the presentation of treatment categories, specialist information and credibility elements.
- **Conversion Journey:** Provides clearer pathways from service discovery to appointment enquiries.
- **Overall Appearance:** Balances the clinic's premium aesthetic identity
